Full study, code and data: https://github.com/frostyhand/can-you-trick-an-ai-shopper

How it worked: 12 product categories with invented brands, matched A/B shelves that differ only in the trick, and five models (Claude Haiku, Sonnet and Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, Gemini 4 Argon). Most numbers above come from "close call" shelves where the products are identical, so small cues decide. Jev never picks a product; it only labels listing text and rates how hard each decision is. Every limitation is written up in the report.
